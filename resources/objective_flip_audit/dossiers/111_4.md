# 111_4 Massassi Base Operations behavior dossier

Family: counted Yavin 4 control gate, permanent back, front-only hard loss, Attack Run payoff.

## Source law

- Front flips when its owner controls at least three Yavin 4 sites and the opponent controls fewer than three. The check includes cards excluded from battle.
- One In A Million has no flip-back trigger. Once the objective flips, the back is permanent.
- While the front is active, Yavin 4 being blown away places the objective out of play. That hard-loss trigger is absent from the back.
- The front may deploy one Yavin 4 site from Reserve Deck each deploy phase. The back may upload one Rebel Tech, Death Star system, Attack Run, or Proton Torpedoes each deploy phase.

Authoritative sources: `Card111_004.java`, `Card111_004_BACK.java`, `Card2_019.java`, `Card2_042.java`, `Card1_158.java`, `Card9_088.java`, and `Card14_066.java`. The objective workbook and public MBO primers informed sequencing only. They are research, not behavioral proof.

## Shipped behavior

- The native front-side site action runs while a legal missing Yavin 4 site remains. Its exact live objective source bypasses the old tiny-Reserve veto because the download is free. A wrong action, wrong source, or same-blueprint counterfeit receives no exception.
- Massassi War Room leads the route because Rebel Tech adds to Attack Run there. The Tech's Trench tutor works at any legal site, so a controlled-site fallback remains executable if War Room is unavailable. Exactly needed route sites and bodies survive Force loss.
- Rando and Chosen One battle for an unmet Yavin control site, leave an anchor behind, move one route actor to the pulled empty site, pull the final site next turn, and deploy the final body there. The actual unchanged card trigger flips the objective.
- The final third-site deployment receives a narrow `DEPLOY.OBJECTIVE.THREE_SITE_COMPLETION +8000` contribution. It dominates the legacy low-ability ground penalty and ordinary ship alternative, while existing hard vetoes remain absolute.
- The permanent back assembles the executable package in prerequisite order: Rebel Tech, Death Star, Attack Run, Proton Torpedoes. It prefers Tech at War Room for the Attack Run bonus but uses a legal controlled-site fallback for the location-independent Trench tutor. It deploys the Death Star and Attack Run, deploys a piloted compatible carrier within one hyperspace move, attaches classic, Enhanced, or Naboo Proton Torpedoes only to their exact legal carrier class, and reserves the real card-modified deployment plus move cost.
- One available Force makes both bots pass instead of attaching Torpedoes and becoming stranded. Two Force pays for Torpedoes and preserves the hyperspace move. Generic weapon-order vetoes are bypassed only for this executable Proton Torpedoes route.
- The armed carrier and sole Trench tutor survive Force loss and battle forfeit while legal fodder exists. Both public bot facades retain parent-to-child provenance for the Torpedoes target and hyperspace destination.
- With the package complete, the exact Attack Run action wins. The unchanged epic event moves the fighter into the Trench, resolves cover, draws two distinct destinies, pulls the fighter back to Death Star, and blows Death Star away. In the deterministic Special Edition Death Star fixture, that resolution creates eight Force loss.

# 7_298 Imperial Occupation behavior dossier

Family: counted operative formations at a runtime-selected planet, with a looser two-site back hold. This is the Dark-side source-law twin of Local Uprising.

## Source law

- Imperial Occupation flips when its owner controls three battleground sites related to the Renegade planet, each with one of that player's matching operatives.
- A matching operative's own ability does not contribute to control on its matching planet. Each counted site therefore needs the matching operative plus another legal source of presence or ability.
- Imperial Control flips back below two occupied Renegade-planet battleground sites. The back requires plain occupation, not operatives or control.
- Setup is ordered by card source. The Objective deploys the planet system first, stores it as the Renegade planet, then appends the required generic-site deployment.
- During later deploy phases, the Objective and an eligible matching operative expose independent once-per-source actions. In the tested route, Dantooine Operative must be at a Dantooine site and may download any site to Dantooine. The Objective downloads only a generic site. No Objective-before-operative order exists.

Authoritative sources: `Card7_298.java`, `Card7_298_BACK.java`, `Card7_174.java`, `OperativesRule.java`, and `LocationControl.java`.

## Implemented behavior

- Both public bots choose Dantooine, supported by three Dantooine Operatives, over unsupported Tatooine, then choose a source-legal generic battleground for setup.
- Imperial Occupation and an on-planet Dantooine Operative use their two independent native pulls in either order to reach three usable route sites. The route survives an Objective-first parser false negative, does not count an opponent-occupied incomplete third site as usable, stops at the profile-defined count, and rejects source-illegal or non-battleground projections.
- Both public bots fund and deploy three one-Force Dantooine Operatives plus three one-Force companions across three complete formations, spend the exact six-Force budget, and fire unchanged `Card7_298` Java's native flip. Engine deployability, actual modified payments, and armed-opponent contact risk are checked before a pair is accepted.
- The shared mirrored planner and evaluators preserve exact physical assignments, battle Force after plan completion, movement Force, the last required formation halves, and the two-site plain-occupation back hold even while a hold site is contested. On the back, an eligible Dantooine Operative can still pull a third legal site as a buffer; the flipped Objective itself cannot.

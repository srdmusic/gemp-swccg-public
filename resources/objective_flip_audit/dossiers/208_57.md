## Source law

- Front: on a table change, flip when the Dark Side controls at least two
  battlegrounds with a physical First Order character at each, including cards
  excluded from battle, and no Resistance Agent owned by either player is
  present at a battleground site.
- A system qualifies. A physical First Order character aboard a starship there
  qualifies. A permanent pilot without a physical First Order character does
  not.
- A Resistance Agent at a system does not block. A Resistance Agent present at
  a battleground site does, including one excluded from battle.
- Back: flip front when the Dark Side occupies fewer than two battlegrounds or
  any Resistance Agent is present at a battleground site. The back uses plain
  occupancy, not control with a First Order character.
- I Will Finish What You Started loses non-Episode VII Dark Jedi for the rest
  of the game. Its Vader clause also loses a deployed Vader or a carrier whose
  permanent pilot is Vader.

Authoritative sources are `Card208_057.java` and `Card208_057_BACK.java`.

## Executable AI route

- Setup retains I Will Finish What You Started as the back-side Interrupt
  anchor. The active profile expresses the front as one exact `allOf` rule and
  the back as one exact `anyOf` rule.
- Starkiller Base `208_51` and The First Order Was Just The Beginning `214_12`
  receive exact battleground-route ownership. Legal route children are favored
  until two qualified controlled battlegrounds exist, then the pull is treated
  as exhausted. Uncontrolled battlegrounds do not falsely exhaust the route.
- Deploy scoring gives an exact missing First Order battleground leg
  `OBJECTIVE.FLIP.BATTLEGROUND_PULL +12000`. A move or carrier-group move only
  counts when it adds a net new qualified battleground rather than trading the
  sole completed leg for another location.
- The bots reject a Reserve Deck deployment that I Will Finish immediately
  loses. They also reject deploying or moving their own declared Resistance
  Agent to a battleground site, which would block their objective.
- Force reserve prices the cheapest currently legal First Order deploy or
  move, plus a legal battle that can clear a Resistance Agent blocker. The
  battle route is selected first and the live battle predictor is called once
  on that exact location. Predictor failure or an unsafe prediction releases
  the battle reserve instead of hoarding Force for a battle the bot will refuse.
- The predictor reads the pre-deploy board. A fight that becomes safe only
  after the current deployment can therefore be conservatively declined. This
  fails toward ordinary deployment, not toward an invented unsafe battle.
- Force-loss and battle-forfeit selection preserve the last physical First
  Order actor, its carrier group, and the two-location hold while an expendable
  alternative exists.
- On the back, both bots take the exact `Stack Interrupt from Lost Pile` and
  `Play stacked Interrupt` actions from the physical objective over Pass.

# 7_137 Local Uprising behavior dossier

Family: counted operative formations at a runtime-selected planet, with a looser two-site back hold.

## Source law

- Local Uprising flips when its owner controls three battleground sites related to the Subjugated planet, each with one of that player's matching operatives.
- A matching operative's own ability does not contribute to control on its matching planet. Each counted site therefore needs the matching operative plus another legal source of presence or ability.
- Liberation flips back below two occupied Subjugated-planet battleground sites. The back requires plain occupation, not operatives or control.
- Setup is ordered by card source. The Objective deploys the planet system first, stores it as the Subjugated planet, then appends the required generic-site deployment.
- During later deploy phases, the Objective and an eligible matching operative expose independent once-per-source actions. In the tested Tibrin route, Tibrin Operative must be at a Tibrin site and may download any site to Tibrin. The Objective downloads only a generic site. Neither card source nor the behavior test requires Objective-first ordering.

Authoritative sources: `Card7_137.java`, `Card7_137_BACK.java`, `Card7_047.java`, `OperativesRule.java`, and `LocationControl.java`.

## Strategy research used

- `Objective_Playbook_Tracker_2026-07-07.xlsx` supplies the fast practical route: choose the supported planet, establish three sites quickly, and place one operative plus one real control source at each site.
- The [Advanced Rulebook](https://res.starwarsccg.org/rules/SWCCG_2023_AdvancedRulebook.pdf) confirms the matching-planet operative restrictions that make the companion mandatory.
- The public [operative rules discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/starwarsccg/comments/fbodvu/rules_advisory_operatives_whoops/) and [1998 World Champion operative list](https://swccgdb.com/decklist/view/5/operatives-1998-world-champion-1.0) explain the historical fast-site sequence and why early deployment tempo matters.
- These materials inform strategy only. Card source and executable tests remain the behavior proof.

## Implemented behavior

- At setup, both public bots choose the planet supported by the greatest number of matching operatives available in hand and Reserve Deck. The tested Tibrin package beats unsupported Dantooine.
- The initial setup-site choice and later native pulls project the exact source-defined deploy-to-system filter and battleground status at the selected planet. A source-illegal site and a legal non-battleground site cannot receive false flip credit.
- The Objective and an on-planet Tibrin Operative use both independent site pulls in either order, reaching three usable qualifying sites in one deploy phase. An opponent-occupied incomplete site does not strand the route, and an exhausted fourth pull is hard-blocked. The operative may supply its pull from any matching-planet site, as printed, while the downloaded destination still must project as a battleground.
- After flip, the Objective no longer supplies a site action, but an eligible operative still steers its legal pull when only two route sites remain. Both public bots choose that action and a third battleground site to buffer Liberation's two-site hold.
- The deploy planner builds three complete operative-plus-companion teams, verifies each hand card against the engine's exact deploy-to-location filter, prices actual modified costs, assigns physical copies to exact sites, and preserves all still-needed Force. It keeps one Force for a winnable contested-site battle even after the pair plan completes, preserves the exact Force for a net-progress landspeed move, and fails closed when a positive deploy payment is unknown. Armed opposing characters reduce projected contact power before the planner accepts a contested pair.
- Movement completes a real pair or preserves an existing pair. Moving one half from a complete pair to another incomplete site is correctly treated as a swap, not new progress.
- Battle, Force-loss, battle-forfeit, and casualty scoring protect the last required operative or legal control companion while expendable alternatives exist. Existing board evaluation accepts source-defined legal companions, including a permanent-pilot vehicle and a nonmatching operative, but a matching operative cannot launder its excluded ability through the vehicle it pilots.
- Post-flip risk logic preserves two plain occupied Subjugated-planet battleground sites without falsely requiring the original operative pairs. That protection remains active at contested sites, where battle casualties and forfeits actually occur. Objective geography alone does not bypass the inherited weak-solo safety rule; only an exact advancing or funded same-site pair does.

## Known limits

- The proactive hand plan deliberately uses non-operative characters as companions. Other legal board presence sources are recognized, but the planner does not proactively deploy every vehicle or Presence-icon package.
- Dark-side movement, battle, loss, forfeit, and post-flip public decisions are not duplicated solely for side symmetry. Those branches are shared and mirrored, while Imperial Occupation has its own public setup, pull, deploy, and native-flip ladder.
- Live replay branch firing remains separate from deterministic proof.

## Source law

- Setup deploys any Coruscant location from Reserve Deck for free.
- While either face is active, the owner's characters whose lore contains `ISB`, `Rebel`, `Rebels`, or `Rebellion` become ISB agents and spies and ignore their own location deployment restrictions.
- Route A flips when four active, spottable ISB agents are anywhere on table. This route is ownership-unrestricted and has no Coruscant or Rebel Base location requirement.
- Route B flips when the owner controls at least two Rebel Base locations, each with one of that owner's active, non-undercover ISB agents there. Rebel Base locations include Hoth and Yavin 4 systems and their related sites, not battleground sites only.
- `SpotOverride.INCLUDE_EXCLUDED_FROM_BATTLE` applies to both native routes. Undercover, captive, or otherwise inactive agents do not count.
- Lieutenant Blount is an external blocker. While he occupies any Coruscant location, ISB Operations may not flip and Empire's Sinister Agents is forcibly flipped to the front. An undercover or excluded-from-battle Blount does not occupy and therefore does not block.
- The back flips to the front when no active, spottable ISB agent remains anywhere on table.
- Once during each owner draw phase, the back may retrieve one ISB agent. A non-undercover ISB agent present at a battleground site gives the owner drain +1 there and reduces the opponent's drains by 1 there and at related locations. The payoff requires presence, not control, and does not multiply for multiple agents.

## Executable behavior

1. The planner compares the native alternatives and reserves Force for the cheapest executable route instead of summing both routes.
2. Route A recognizes the exact objective-granted lore class, protects four distinct on-table agents, and permits deployment at any legal destination.
3. Route B steers qualifying agents to two distinct Rebel Base locations, preserves an origin that must remain controlled, reserves legal movement and favorable-battle costs, and completes only the source-defined control-with formation.
4. Completion deployment receives the semantic `OBJECTIVE.ISB.ROUTE_COMPLETION +8000` score. The old V176 battle-reserve suppression is released only for an exact flip-completing ISB deploy.
5. When Blount is the only unmet blocker, both bots reserve a safe one-hop Coruscant landspeed move, choose his exact site, and may take a formation-safe battle with a strict power edge. Existing movement scoring supplies `+600` for the chase parent and `+1000` for the exact destination.
6. Force loss and battle forfeit retain the last required route pieces. Route A protection includes an agent aboard an enclosed vehicle even when `presentAt` is null. Ordinary expendable cards remain eligible first.
7. On the back, the native free retrieval receives `OBJECTIVE.ISB.RETRIEVE_AGENT +2000`, and non-undercover agents are steered toward battleground sites for the printed drain payoff while the last active agent remains protected.

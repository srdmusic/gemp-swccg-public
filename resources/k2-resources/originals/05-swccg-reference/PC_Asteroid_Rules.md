Asteroid Rules
Wherever "Asteroid Rules" are in effect, your starships there are continually subject to being hit by asteroids.
During each of your control phases, for each such starship:
Opponent draws one asteroid destiny.
Add 1 for each additional sector at that system that has "Asteroid Rules" in effect.
If total asteroid destiny > armor or maneuver, the starship is lost.
If the card drawn for asteroid destiny is itself an asteroid sector, the starship is lost, no matter how high its
armor or maneuver.
